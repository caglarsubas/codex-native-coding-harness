## Design Context

Native permission requests must be visible from every workspace section, not
only Brain chat. Use one compact, persistent notice and open the exact owner-only
review in the same brain inspector. On narrow screens, keep the notice inside
the modal sheet's focus boundary. Never steal focus when a request arrives.
Distinguish the owned connection's waiting lifetime from the short-lived signed
confirmation preview; expired previews should explain that nothing was sent
and allow explicit re-review only if the same native request is still pending.

When a lost observer leaves an empty standard brain turn stranded, show
**Review turn recovery** in that same inspector. One unchecked exact confirmation
covers conditional cancellation and verified controller recovery, not another
permission or Play. Show retained progress, a read-only check after uncertainty,
and the existing checkpoint review after recovery. No long prompt-writing detour,
implied checkpoint, automatic native effect or focus stealing.

### Users
The repository owner uses this local tool inside Codex to manage approved work
across repositories, inspect worker evidence, and understand what needs a decision.
It is the workspace control surface for Codex, including durable project-brain
conversation. Keep that conversation distinct from the advisory inference panel
and never imply it is a complete native task transcript.

### Brand Personality
Assured, precise, composed: carried forward from the existing Harness design
context. Use truthful freshness and explicit unknown states.

### Aesthetic Direction
An assistant-led development cycle: Plan, Review & Play, Develop, Checkpoint.
Keep this sequence in one graph-centered project workspace, with a docked
inspector for durable brain conversation, tasks, roadmap controls and evidence.
Lead with the recorded state and one useful next action. The brain-to-task graph
is the persistent spatial anchor, not a separate monitoring page. Remove the
persistent navigation rail and competing assistant pane; keep the advisory guide
visibly distinct inside contextual inspection. Use restrained forest-green actions,
ink and warm paper surfaces. Group advanced controls and measurements behind
progressive disclosure. Light and dark themes. Local
font availability only; no external assets or font downloads. Optima headings
paired with Avenir Next body type, with locally available fallbacks.

### Design Principles
1. Show authority, state freshness and next action before decorative metrics.
2. Queued requests and completed native actions are visibly different.
3. Evidence axes remain separate and inspectable.
4. Tables and controls work with keyboard, narrow panels and reduced motion.
5. Graph edges describe recorded responsibility; animate only fresh observed activity.
6. No placeholder healthy data or invented usage; completion and archival stay separate.
7. Recovery, optional brain replacement and historical evidence do not compete
   with the normal phase loop. Explain a prerequisite with an actionable next
   step instead of offering an unexplained disabled Play button.
8. Summary first throughout the platform: concise outcome bullets and the next
   action; full narrative behind Details. Never hide approval scope or blockers.
9. Resolve consecutive prerequisites in chat. Present exact confirmations and
   retained outcomes inline; do not require page navigation for the normal phase loop.
10. Preserve old deep links as inspector selections and keep the graph visible
    across refreshes. On narrow screens, use a dismissible inspector sheet with
    the same controls and evidence, not a reduced-function mobile variant.
11. A finished recovery must expose its exact next transition in place. Distinguish
    closing an unqualified stopped phase from preparing, reviewing and playing
    its successor; never send the owner to a dead-end controls page.
12. Keep expired reviews readable and refreshable in place. Show confirmation
    expiry, phase expiry and host connection separately; configuration and old
    process records never prove live connectivity.
